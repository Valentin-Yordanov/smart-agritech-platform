# Agronomic Guide: Soil Health, pH & Nutrient Deficiency Management

## Overview & The Law of the Minimum
Plant growth and disease resistance are strictly constrained by the single most limiting nutrient factor (Liebig's Law of the Minimum). Soil physicochemical balance governs nutrient availability, root architecture, microbial symbiosis, and crop resilience against foliar and vascular pathogens.

## Soil pH & Nutrient Bioavailability
- **Optimal Range:** Most agronomic and horticultural crops achieve peak nutrient uptake at a slightly acidic to neutral soil pH of 6.2 to 6.8.
- **Acidic Soils (pH < 5.8):** Induces aluminum (Al) and manganese (Mn) phytotoxicity while severely locking up phosphorus (P), calcium (Ca), and magnesium (Mg). Correct by applying agricultural calcitic or dolomitic limestone based on buffer pH test.
- **Alkaline Soils (pH > 7.5):** Strongly precipitates phosphorus with calcium into insoluble calcium phosphates, and triggers induced micronutrient chlorosis—particularly Iron (Fe), Zinc (Zn), and Manganese (Mn). Ameliorate using elemental sulfur, ammonium sulfate fertilizers, or foliar chelated iron (Fe-EDDHA).

## Key Nutrient Deficiencies & Diagnostic Symptoms

### Primary Macronutrients (N, P, K)
1. **Nitrogen (N) - Mobile:**
   - *Deficiency:* General uniform chlorosis (pale yellowing) initiating on older lower leaves while newer growth remains light green. Stunted spindly stems and premature leaf drop.
   - *Excess:* Abundant lush dark green foliage, delayed flowering and fruiting, weak stems prone to lodging, increased susceptibility to sucking pests (aphids, mites) and fungal pathogens.
2. **Phosphorus (P) - Mobile:**
   - *Deficiency:* Stunted growth, dark bluish-green foliage with intense purple or reddish anthocyanin coloration along lower leaf veins and underside petioles. Restricted root mass.
   - *Remediation:* Banding monoammonium phosphate (MAP) or triple superphosphate (TSP); compost/bone meal in organic systems.
3. **Potassium (K) - Mobile:**
   - *Deficiency:* Marginal scorch and chlorosis (yellowing/browning along leaf edges) starting on mature leaves while center veins remain green. Poor drought tolerance, weak stems, and uneven fruit ripening.
   - *Remediation:* Potassium sulfate (K2SO4) or potassium chloride (MOP).

### Secondary Macronutrients (Ca, Mg, S)
1. **Calcium (Ca) - Immobile:**
   - *Deficiency:* Manifests at growing points and active cell division zones. Typical disorders include Blossom-End Rot (BER) in tomatoes and peppers, tipburn in lettuce, and bitter pit in apples. New leaves become distorted, hooked, or necrotic at tips.
   - *Root Cause:* Usually water fluctuation rather than absolute soil calcium deficit; calcium moves exclusively via the transpiration stream in the xylem.
   - *Remediation:* Stabilize drip irrigation regimes; apply gypsum (calcium sulfate) or foliar calcium nitrate sprays.
2. **Magnesium (Mg) - Mobile:**
   - *Deficiency:* Sharp interveinal chlorosis on older leaves (green veins with striking yellow or pale tissue between veins), often turning brittle with necrotic margins.
   - *Remediation:* Foliar or soil application of Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate, MgSO4).

### Key Micronutrients (Fe, Zn, B)
1. **Iron (Fe) - Immobile:**
   - *Deficiency:* Distinct ivory to bright yellow interveinal chlorosis on the youngest upper leaves, while veins remain prominently green. Common in calcareous high-pH soils.
   - *Remediation:* Foliar iron chelate (Fe-EDTA for pH < 6.5; Fe-EDDHA for alkaline soils up to pH 9.0).
2. **Boron (B) - Immobile:**
   - *Deficiency:* Brittle stems, hollow stems in brassicas, poor pollen viability, flower abortion, and cracked fruit surfaces.

## Soil Organic Matter (SOM) & Regenerative Practices
- **Soil Biological Fertility:** Maintain SOM > 3.0% through green manures (clover, vetch, rye), well-cured compost, and minimal tillage.
- **Mycorrhizal Inoculation:** Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) expand effective root surface area by up to 100x, substantially enhancing phosphorus and micronutrient foraging under dry spells.
